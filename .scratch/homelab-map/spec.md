# Interactive homelab map

Status: complete

## Scope

Extend the portfolio with a polished, interactive, publicly safe explanation of the real homelab. Evidence comes from the local SandeepCloud-IAC/Homelab repositories and Obsidian project notes, inspected November 4, 2026. Visitor mode: Experience. The user approved the navy/gold diagram, clickable services, traffic animation, and stacked mobile layout.

Placement: immediately before Projects, after Skills, per the user's explicit follow-up. Navigation follows the same section order.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Let visitors follow a real request through Sandeep's infrastructure and inspect the decisions behind it.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the site's navy surfaces, gold selection, Outfit headings, Inter body, rounded controls, and fine borders. Use crisp topology geometry, not decorative illustrations.

STORY: Choose a traffic route, follow its highlighted connections, then select a service for its purpose, boundary, and public evidence.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Heading and short introduction above route controls. The diagram occupies the left two-thirds, with a readable inspector on the right. A visibly bounded physical host contains the separate Hyper-V, WSL, and native Windows environments. External services sit outside it. Phones stack the environments and insert selected details directly after their node.

FORM: Precisely specified local extension; no concept seed or replacement visual world. Signature interaction: selecting a route highlights its nodes and directional line animation; selecting a service updates its inspector. Motion can be paused and is suspended off-screen.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
